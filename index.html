Rockcity Privacy Policy

Effective date: 10 September 2026
Last updated: 10 September 2026
Operator: Nnamdi Nwali (“we”, “us”, or “our”)
Application: Rockcity (package name: com.rockcity.game)

Rockcity is a play-to-earn platform where users can play games, complete milestones, track progress, and, where available, earn rewards that may be withdrawn according to the rules shown in the app. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Scope

This Policy applies to the Rockcity mobile application, the Rockcity website (including pages hosted at nnamdinwali.github.io/rockcity), and related services used to operate accounts, gameplay tracking, rewards, and support.

2. Information we collect

We may collect the following categories of information:

  • Account information — such as name, email address, profile image, and sign-in identifiers when you register or log in (including through authentication providers such as Clerk and Google Sign-In).
  • Gameplay and progress data — such as games played, sessions, scores, milestone completion, rewards earned, leaderboard standing, and similar activity needed to operate play-to-earn features.
  • Transaction and payout data — where cash-out or reward withdrawal is offered, information needed to process requests (for example payout method details you provide, amounts requested, and status of requests). We do not ask for unnecessary financial data.
  • Device and technical data — such as device type, operating system, app version, approximate network information, crash logs, and diagnostic data to keep the service secure and stable.
  • Usage data — pages or screens viewed, buttons tapped, referral codes if used, and similar interaction data.
  • Support communications — messages you send to us and related correspondence.

We do not knowingly require sensitive personal data (such as government ID) unless a specific verification step is required by law or by a payout partner, in which case you will be told at that time.

3. How we use information

  • To create and manage your Rockcity account
  • To operate gameplay, milestones, rewards, leaderboards, and related features
  • To calculate, display, and process earnings and cash-out requests where available
  • To authenticate you and prevent fraud, abuse, multi-accounting, and reward manipulation
  • To improve performance, fix bugs, and understand how the platform is used
  • To send service-related notices (for example security, account, or payout updates)
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Legal bases (where applicable)

Depending on your location, we process personal data because it is necessary to provide the service you requested (contract), to pursue legitimate interests such as security and product improvement, to comply with law, or with your consent where required.

5. Authentication providers

Rockcity uses third-party authentication services (including Clerk and, when you choose it, Google Sign-In). Those providers process information according to their own privacy policies. When you sign in with Google, Google may share basic profile information (such as name and email) with Clerk/Rockcity to create or access your account.

6. Sharing of information

We may share information only as needed with:

  • Service providers that help us host, authenticate, store data, send notifications, analyze stability, or process payouts
  • Fraud-prevention and security partners when required to protect users and the platform
  • Authorities when required by law, court order, or to protect rights and safety
  • Business transfers if we reorganize, merge, or transfer assets, subject to appropriate protections

We require parties who process personal data for us to protect it appropriately.

7. Rewards, milestones, and play-to-earn features

Progress, milestone claims, balances, and related records are stored so we can operate the earn features fairly. We may review activity for abuse (for example fake installs, automated play, or policy violations). Rewards and cash-outs are subject to the rules, limits, and eligibility shown in the app and may change.

8. Advertising and analytics

If the app or website shows ads or uses analytics, those partners may collect device and usage data under their own policies. Where required, we will provide in-app disclosure or consent. You can limit ad personalization through your device settings (for example Google Play’s ad settings on Android).

9. Data retention

We keep account and gameplay records for as long as your account remains active and as needed to provide the service, resolve disputes, prevent fraud, and meet legal requirements. You may request deletion of your account as described below; some records may be retained in limited form where the law requires or where needed for security.

10. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal information. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. Please protect your login credentials and device access.

11. Children’s privacy

Rockcity is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will take appropriate steps to delete it.

12. International users

Your information may be processed in countries other than your own, including where our service providers operate. Those countries may have different data-protection laws. We take steps designed to protect your information in line with this Policy.

13. Your rights and choices

Depending on your location, you may have rights to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your account or certain data
  • Object to or restrict certain processing
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent
  • Export a copy of data you provided (data portability), where applicable

To make a request, contact us using the details below. We may need to verify your identity before fulfilling the request.

14. Third-party links and games

Rockcity may link to third-party games, offerwalls, websites, or services. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies. We are not responsible for third-party content or practices outside our control.

15. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of Rockcity after an update means you accept the revised Policy, except where additional consent is required by law.
